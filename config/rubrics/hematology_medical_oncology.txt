TOPIC: Hematology & Medical Oncology (heme/onc)
Score 0.0–1.0 for how central a HEMATOLOGIC MALIGNANCY or the SYSTEMIC CLINICAL
MANAGEMENT OF CANCER is to the paper's PRIMARY contribution.

DECISION ORDER — apply top-down, first match wins:

1. Is the paper FUNDAMENTALLY a method/assay/screen — i.e. its unit of
   contribution is the assay, molecule, or model itself (developing/validating a
   biochemical assay e.g. TR-FRET/binding/reporter, a small-molecule screen, a
   detection/biomarker/cfDNA/sequencing assay, or an ML/computational model)?
   -> NOT heme/onc, EVEN IF the target or sample is a cancer (incl. AML, LILRB4).
      Route: drug_discovery_pharmacology (screens/assays) OR
      biomedical_informatics / the organ cancer topic (detection biomarkers).
   NOTE: a paper that merely USES an assay to make a clinical claim about a
   malignancy (e.g. flow-cytometry MRD predicts AML relapse) is NOT a method
   paper — judge by the contribution, not the technique.

2. Is the disease a BENIGN blood disorder (anemia, sickle cell, thalassemia/
   hemoglobinopathy, hemostasis/thrombosis, erythropoiesis, iron balance)?
   -> NOT heme/onc. Route: hematology. A gene/cell therapy (e.g. exa-cel) for a
      hemoglobinopathy STAYS here — the non-malignant disease wins over the
      "cellular therapy / stem cell" wording.

3. Is the primary subject a NAMED HEMATOLOGIC MALIGNANCY (AML, ALL, CML, CLL,
   Hodgkin/NH lymphoma, multiple myeloma, MDS, MPN + subtypes) — including its
   cytogenetics, molecular subtypes, risk stratification, prognosis, or therapy?
   -> heme/onc. Heme malignancies have NO separate biology sibling; naming the
      disease is sufficient. AML karyotype/deletion work is heme/onc, NOT
      cancer_biology_general.

4. Is the primary subject SYSTEMIC MEDICAL-ONCOLOGY PRACTICE cutting across organ
   sites — a chemo/immuno/targeted-therapy regimen or drug CLASS, an oncology
   trial's design/conduct, CAR-T or stem-cell transplant FOR A MALIGNANCY, or
   supportive/survivorship care for cancer patients? -> heme/onc.

5. Is the primary subject a SOLID TUMOR anchored to one organ (its biology,
   organ-specific biomarkers, or a therapy studied only in that organ)?
   -> NOT heme/onc; route to the organ sibling (breast/lung/GI/GU/neuro/gyn/
      melanoma) or cancer_biology_general.
   SPECIFICITY RULE: when a systemic therapy is studied in a SINGLE organ tumor,
   the organ sibling wins; send to heme/onc only if the contribution is about the
   therapy/regimen/trial ITSELF, not the organ disease.

DISQUALIFIERS that "sound" onco but score ~0:
  - cellular-therapy / "stem cell" / transplant language with a NON-malignant
    disease (exa-cel for thalassemia; CAR-T for lupus) — disease wins (rule 2).
  - "cancer" present only as a detection target, comorbidity, incidental cohort,
    or dataset — the contribution lives elsewhere.
  - a malignancy-relevant target studied via a bench/screening assay — the ASSAY
    nature wins (rule 1), regardless of the cancer link.

SCORE BANDS (example phrasings):
  0.0  No heme-malignancy and no systemic-oncology-practice focus; belongs to a sibling.
       - "CRISPR exa-cel restores erythropoiesis in β-thalassemia" (hematology + gene_cell_therapy)
       - "TR-FRET assay for small-molecule screening of LILRB4" (drug_discovery_pharmacology)
       - "cfDNA methylation assay detects colorectal cancer" (gi_cancer / biomedical_informatics)
  0.3  Cancer/heme present but background, comorbidity, or incidental cohort/dataset;
       contribution is in another field.
       - "Cardiac outcomes in a cohort that included lymphoma survivors"
       - "Anesthesia protocol for patients undergoing tumor resection"
  0.6  A heme malignancy or systemic-oncology topic is a CORE theme, but the contribution
       is descriptive/characterizing or shares focus.
       - "Chromosomal deletions 11q/13/13q in AML with noncomplex karyotypes"
       - "Incidence and subtype trends of multiple myeloma"
  0.9  The CENTRAL contribution is the diagnosis, risk-stratification, or systemic
       management of a heme malignancy or medical-oncology practice.
       - "Molecular remission and transplant decisions in Ph+ ALL"
       - "Phase III trial of a novel induction regimen in relapsed AML"
